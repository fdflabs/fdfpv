## The arc

A new drone detachment is sent north to a vast rural region, the
Interior, officially to update infrastructure imagery. A small armed
movement called the Column was supposed to be a relic. Watching long
enough, the players find it was never gone, only changing: an old
generation trying to survive and keep its memory, a young one being
absorbed by a moneyed outfit with no ideology, the Network. The campaign
ends not on a victory but on a choice about what to do with the truth.

Recognisably northern Paraguay; every place, person and group invented
(BIBLE 2: Río Sereno, Colonia Arroyo Manso, Puente Doble, Monte Cerrado,
Claro Viejo, Estancia La Ceniza, Puesto Arenal, La Pista Larga, Rincón
Quemado and the rest). People are seen from the air; the operations room
is told on its monitors, maps and stills, never a face. Four voices:
Vega (command), Ibarra (the analyst), Rojas (the ground), Ferrer
(systems).

## What is built now and what later

**Now: Phase 0 and Mission 1, to 100 %.** Phase 0 builds only what
Mission 1 needs (TECH-NEEDS section 3, rows marked P0): the M1 corridor
of the map, canopy that hides people, people and two vehicles on

quiet HUD, the debrief over the squad's own stills, the room owned
contact registry, a camera ball, the role system, the BOARD for the
films, the four voices, the campaign card listing all five. Then Mission

it; only then does it open.

**Later, one at a time:** Missions 2 to 5, each from its plan here,
opening only when its story, films and stages are done.

## Any number of pilots, roles and guides

Your addendum, applied to every mission (MISSIONS 1.5): one pilot or a
full room fly the same mission. Roles are dealt at random at the start
(ISR, tracker, recon quad, relay, strike, interceptor, ground support);
anyone can take a free role or swap with another pilot at any time,
except during a few locked beats; the host can lock roles. Each role has
its own guide on the radio (Ibarra for finding and following, Ferrer for
relay and interceptors, Rojas for the ground and strikes) and its own
objective cards; Vega's calls and the story are heard by everyone. Extra
pilots get scaling roles that do real work (another search box, a second
track, another interceptor), never an idle seat. More pilots change
coverage and pressure, never the story. The room holds 8 pilots in a
private room and 16 in a public one today; the missions have no upper
limit of their own.

These modules (roles, capture, the quiet HUD, cards, guides) are built
for any campaign, so Defend the Paraná can adopt them once The Interior
has proved them (your note: the Interior sets the standard). LESSONS.md
records what each phase teaches.
